Lives at the top of your screen.

Sutra Island stays compact until you need it, then expands into whatever is happening now.

  • Media and audio

    Album art, playback controls, seek, ±30-second skips, volume, output switching and a live equalizer.

  • At a glance

    Clock, date, battery, memory, network, weather, and AirPods or compatible Beats battery.

  • Timers and alarms

    Named timers, alarms, a stopwatch, and a compact timer activity.

  • Screenshot tool

    Ctrl+Alt+S opens region, window or full-screen capture. Copy, save PNG, draw, undo or pin.

  • Sutra Flow

    Hold Right Ctrl and talk. Your words are typed into any app, transcribed on your PC.

  • Sutra Assistant

    Typed chat, "Hey Sutra" voice, and screen help only when you ask for it.

  • Yours to tune

    Choose activities, appearance, position, shortcuts and reduced motion.

Hold a key. Talk. Let go.

Sutra Flow types what you say into whatever app you're in. Hold Right Ctrl, speak, release, and clean text lands where your cursor is.

  • Runs on your PC

    Speech recognition uses Whisper on your own machine. Your audio never leaves it.

  • Cleans up as you go

    Drops um and uh, stutters, and spoken punctuation like "comma" and "new line". Add your own AI key and it also catches "Monday, no wait, Tuesday" and formats lists.

  • Turns your music down

    Other apps drop the moment you press the key and come back when you let go. Set how far, from mute to 100%.

  • Pastes safely

    Text only goes into the field you started in, and your clipboard is put back exactly as it was.

  • Learns your words

    Add names to a vocabulary, or fix a word once after it pastes and Flow remembers it.

  • Speaks code

    Say "camel case get user by id" in VS Code, Cursor or Windsurf, or "at index dot tsx" to tag a file in chat.

An assistant that waits to be asked.

Pick your provider and model in Settings. Ordinary chat never captures your screen; choose Use screen for the next question, or call screen help on purpose.

Or just say "Hey Sutra." The wake word and voice input now run on the same local Whisper engine as Flow, with adjustable wake sensitivity, and your music steps aside while you talk.
